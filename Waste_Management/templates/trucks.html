Capacity & Assignment Rules

  • Trucks never receive assignments exceeding their maximum capacity
  • Multi-compartment trucks can carry recyclable, organic, and general waste simultaneously
  • Chemical and hazardous waste requires specialized vehicle T04 — other trucks will be rejected
  • Utilization is tracked per trip (e.g., 4,200 kg / 5,000 kg = 84% utilized)

Safety & Compliance

⚠ Chemical & Hazardous Waste: These categories are never mixed with regular waste. They are always assigned to the specialized HazChem Special Vehicle (T04) and routed directly to the appropriate treatment/disposal facility. Attempting to assign them to an ordinary truck will result in a safety violation warning.
